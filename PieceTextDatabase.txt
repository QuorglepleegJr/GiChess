Piece 006
**Pawn**
Tier 1
Cost 1

- **Promotable**
- Moves 1 square forward (without capturing). The first time you move **Pawn**, you may move it two squares forward (without capturing) instead.
- Captures 1 square diagonally forward.
- **Pawn**s can perform *En Passant*.

Mechanic Definitions:
*En Passant*: The turn after a **Tier 1** piece that can move extra squares the first time it is moved moves that way, a piece able to perform *En Passant* that could have captured it had it moved its normal distance can capture it. The capturing piece moves to the square the other piece would have moved to had it moved its normal distance.

Piece 009
**Poisoner**
Tier 3
Cost 12

- **Unique**
- Moves like an 001 **King**.
- At the beginning of your turn, on **Cooldown** 3, **Uncharged**: choose a non-**Important** enemy piece on the board (different to last turn). That piece is *Poisoned* until your next turn.
- **Poisoner** is *Healthy*.

*Poisoned*, *Healthy*: *Poisoned* pieces lose all abilities and are *Slowed*. If a piece *Poisons* itself, it remains *Poisoned* until it would normally wear off. A piece that is *Healthy* is not *Poisoned* and can't be *Poisoned*.
*Slowed*: *Slowed* pieces can move only half their specified movement distance during their turn. *Slowed* pieces with infinite range can only move up to one quarter of the board (rounding up), and pieces with 1-tile movement cannot move if they moved last turn. *Slowed* pieces can only make moves they normally could.

Notation:
`Psn(enemy piece location)`

Piece 041
**Stapler**
Relic
Cost 5

- At the beginning of the game: choose a file, **Stapler** affects the two middle square of that file.
  - If there are an odd number of ranks, choose one side of the middle square of that file to be affected alongside the middle square.
  - You may not choose a file that has any pieces on the affected tiles.
- The two squares **Stapler** affects are considered 1 square for all purposes.
  - As with any individual square, the joined square may only contain 1 piece unless specified otherwise.
  - Orthogonal adjacencies are the orthogonal adjacencies of either square (excluding the joined squares).
  - Diagonal adjacencies are the diagonal adjacencies of either square that aren't orthogonal adjacencies.
  - The joined square is considered to be the colours of both of its component squares.

Notation:
The joined square is referred to as `([location1][location2])`, e.g. `(e4e5)`.
